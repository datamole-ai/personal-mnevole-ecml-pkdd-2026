Conference talk on a subjective, perception-aware navigation system deployed in Kyoto that uses a photo-posting app and a quest-based "hunting" app to collect and maintain urban impression data.

### System & Research Questions
- Two components: posting app (geotagged photos + impressions) and hunting app (quest-based navigation)
- RQs: does long-term collection reduce spatial bias, and does navigation motivate exploration

### Deployment & Findings
- Deployed in Kyoto 2024-2026: 8,000+ geotagged photos, 2,000+ impression entries
- Data still long-tailed, concentrated on major roads even after two seasons
- 25/32 participants completed streak; revisits cluster at short-term and 1-day intervals
- Simple signal-based rules flagged 6/8 high-risk cells; multi-dimensional signals catch all

### Q&A
- Updates shared only with nearby users; temporary/time-bound events not yet handled
- No socio-demographic data collected on participants currently
